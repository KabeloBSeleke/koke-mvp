How we work

Six phases that take a programme from scope to documented close-out — with quality and compliance built in at every step.

  1. 01

    Define

    Confirm scope and targets: what will be delivered, where and by when.

  2. 02

    Mobilise

    Set up the management unit, roles, governance and reporting lines.

  3. 03

    Procure and contract

    Appoint service providers in line with public finance rules and sign agreements.

  4. 04

    Implement

    Coordinate design, delivery and quality checks across all sites.

  5. 05

    Build capacity

    Train client staff and local partners so the work continues after the project.

  6. 06

    Monitor and evaluate

    Track progress continuously, report regularly and close out with a documented evaluation.

Quality and compliance

Applied across every project we run.

  • ✓Service providers appointed in line with public finance supply chain procedures.
  • ✓Quality management system applied across projects.
  • ✓Health and safety checks on training venues before learners start.
  • ✓Learner records registered on SETA systems, with internal and external moderation and QCTO submission.
  • ✓Ongoing monitoring and evaluation with site inspections and learner feedback.
