About

Who we are

A project management company that acts as the management unit on publicly funded programmes, with a focus on skills development.

Story

Our story

Koke Holdings (Pty) Ltd was registered in 2017 and is based in Boitumelong, Bloemhof, in the North West. We are a project management company that acts as the management unit on publicly funded programmes, with a focus on skills development: learnerships, internships and artisan pathways. Our role is to bring structure to complex programmes. We mobilise teams, coordinate service providers, uphold quality standards and report clearly on results, so our clients always know what is being delivered, where and by when.

Mission, vision and values

Mission

To plan, govern and deliver publicly funded programmes that create clear, measurable benefits for communities: on time, on budget and in line with South African laws and policies.

Vision

Programmes that leave lasting skills and capacity in the communities they serve.

Values

Accountability to our clients, compliance with public finance and quality standards, transparent reporting, and building capacity that lasts beyond the project.

What makes us different

We work as the management unit, not as one more service provider: a single point of coordination across clients, training providers and host workplaces. Compliance is built in from the start, from procurement in line with public finance rules to SETA registration and QCTO evidence. And we plan for close-out from day one, training client staff and local partners so the work continues after we leave.
