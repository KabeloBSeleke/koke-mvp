About & Governance

Koke Holdings (Pty) Ltd operates as the appointed Project Management Service Provider, working alongside the client's internal oversight structure and a dedicated Project Steering Committee.

Since public-sector clients cannot hold SAQA accreditation directly, Koke Holdings designates and manages qualified, accredited service providers to deliver programmes compliantly under PFMA supply chain procedures.

Our proposed PMU structure: Managing Director → Programme Manager, supported by Programme Support and a Finance Manager, with Project Managers assigned per district/region (DRSM, DKK, Ngaka Modiri, Bojanala).

PFMA SAQA QCTO SETA / LMIS
“Since the applicant is a government agency, it is not allowed to get accreditation under SAQA requirements. Instead, the applicant will designate qualified service providers to carry out the suggested programs in accordance with the PFMA supply chain procedures.” Governance Framework
“To measure the project’s efficacy and progress, we shall create strong monitoring and evaluation systems throughout the duration of the project. This covers performance evaluations, site inspections by designated M&E officers, and routine reporting — including speaking with students one-on-one to find out how they view project development.” M&E Philosophy
