Services

What we do

We run the management unit behind publicly funded programmes — from set-up and procurement to delivery, quality and reporting.

01

Programme and project management

Project office set-up, governance structures, communications, and coordination from kick-off to closure.

02

Skills development programme management

End-to-end management of learnerships, internships and artisan pathways, from recruitment to certification.

03

Procurement and contracting support

Helping clients appoint accredited training providers and other service providers in line with public finance rules.

04

Stakeholder engagement

Identifying stakeholders, clarifying roles and coordinating host workplaces such as departments, municipalities and private sector partners.

05

Quality management and compliance

Quality management systems, training venue readiness, health and safety checks, SETA registration and QCTO evidence submission.

06

Monitoring, evaluation and reporting

Continuous monitoring, site inspections, learner feedback, and narrative and evaluation reporting for clients and funders.

07

Capacity building and exit strategy

Training client staff and local partners for continuity, capturing lessons learned and structured close-out.

Method

How we work

A simple, repeatable approach — from defining the programme to a documented close-out.

  1. 01

    Define

    Confirm scope and targets: what will be delivered, where and by when.

  2. 02

    Mobilise

    Set up the management unit, roles, governance and reporting lines.

  3. 03

    Procure and contract

    Appoint service providers in line with public finance rules and sign agreements.

  4. 04

    Implement

    Coordinate design, delivery and quality checks across all sites.

  5. 05

    Build capacity

    Train client staff and local partners so the work continues after the project.

  6. 06

    Monitor and evaluate

    Track progress continuously, report regularly and close out with a documented evaluation.
